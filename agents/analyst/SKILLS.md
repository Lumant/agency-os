## Measurement Frameworks

### Marketing KPIs by Channel
- **Social media:** reach, impressions, engagement rate, follower growth, click-through rate, saves, shares
- **Email:** open rate, click rate, unsubscribe rate, conversion rate
- **Website:** sessions, unique users, bounce rate, time on page, goal completions
- **E-commerce:** conversion rate, average order value, revenue per visitor, cart abandonment rate
- **Paid:** CPM, CPC, CTR, ROAS, CPA

### Experimentation Basics
- A/B test design: one variable at a time, sufficient sample size, clear hypothesis
- Statistical significance: when results are trustworthy vs. noise
- Multivariate testing: when appropriate vs. when to simplify
- Cohort analysis: comparing groups over time

### Critical Thinking Tools
- Pre-mortem: "Assume this failed. What went wrong?"
- Assumption mapping: explicit vs. implicit assumptions, high vs. low risk
- Second-order effects: what happens after the immediate result?
