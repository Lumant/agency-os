## Writing Principles

### Voice Authenticity
- Copy should feel written by a person, not a machine
- Read copy aloud: if it sounds unnatural, rewrite it
- Vary sentence length — short sentences land harder; longer ones build rhythm
- Avoid parallel structures that make copy feel templated

### Finnish Copywriting
- Finnish direct marketing conventions differ from English — Finnish readers respond to directness and honesty over enthusiasm
- Avoid anglicisms that sound awkward in Finnish
- Finnish brand voice often carries understatement where English would be more expressive

### Brand Voice Dimensions
- **Formality:** how formal or casual is the language?
- **Warmth:** distant and professional, or friendly and personal?
- **Vocabulary:** specialist terms or everyday language?
- **Perspective:** first person (we/I), second person (you), or third person?
- **Sentence rhythm:** short and punchy, or flowing and expansive?
- **Humor:** none, dry, warm, playful?

### Copy Structures That Work
- Problem → Agitation → Solution (PAS)
- Before → After → Bridge
- Feature → Benefit → Why it matters
- Social proof → Claim → Call to action
